Before you begin

  • File format: Set up your spreadsheet with one design per row. Every column that holds a number should be either one of your objectives (like cost or carbon) or a design variable (like an insulation level or window type) — the tool will ask you to label each one after you upload.
  • Your data stays private: This tool does all of its work directly inside your web browser. That means your spreadsheet is never sent to, or stored on, any outside server — not even ours. Closing the page clears everything.
